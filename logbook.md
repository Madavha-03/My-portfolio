## Reflection

*[DRAFT: edit in your own words]* The logbook taught me that a stand-up record is only as valuable as it is timely. Our entries show the rhythm of the project, with the same three questions every day exposing impediments early, for example the GitHub authentication and repository permissions problem and the payment callback security issue near the end.

It also showed me a weakness in our process. Several periods had to be reconstructed after the fact, and many entries still need their evidence linked. In future I would write the entry the same day, attach the commit or design link at that moment, and use the logbook as a live tool for the team rather than a record built later.
