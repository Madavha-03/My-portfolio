# Agile Project Portfolio

*PRP370-1-2S | CPUT ICT Networking | 2026*

This portfolio presents my Agile project evidence for **CareerCraft**, our Entrepreneurial WIL project: an AI-assisted career platform for South African students and graduates. The team (Lwandile Mangali, Sello Makhunyane, Lerato Motsweni and me, Livhuwani Madavha) worked from 1 June to 30 September 2026 in 4 initiation weeks followed by 14 weekly sprints. My role was UX/UI Designer and Front-end Developer.

- [Agile Team Charter](team-charter.html): purpose, roles, values, communication, goals, risks
- [User Stories and Tasks](user-stories-and-tasks.html): 18 stories with owned tasks and acceptance criteria
- [Product Backlog](product-backlog.html): all backlog items in delivery order, plus deferred work
- [Sprint Backlog](sprint-backlog.html): sprint goals, selected tasks, outcomes and retrospectives

## A note on evidence

> **Evidence status.** Periods marked **"Reconstructed – team confirmation required"** were written up after the fact from project evidence and are not yet confirmed by the whole team. Where an evidence field says "To verify", the supporting commit, design version or test record still needs to be linked. These labels are kept so the record stays accurate.

Of 18 periods, 14 carry the "Reconstructed" label; the rest are marked "Documented in the supplied logbook".
